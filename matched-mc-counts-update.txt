Observed rough-Heston tail-sampling noise

At the shortest calibrated maturity, T=0.019178, the independent 2-million-path
sample gives a zero price for K=120 in 7 of the 100 Legendre runs: 28, 32, 63,
74, 77, 93, and 95. These imply zero estimated implied volatility for that quote
and increase the aggregate MAE and its standard deviation. All seven observations
are included in the reported statistics. No prices or errors were discarded,
replaced, or assigned a different evaluation seed.

A direct check of runs 28 and 74 confirmed that none of the 2 million sampled
terminal prices exceeded 120 (sample maxima 118.52857 and 116.70252). These are
zero Monte Carlo estimates from rare exercise events, not a claim that the
model's exact option price or exact implied volatility is zero. The manuscript
caption and the rough-Heston study notebook explicitly report the seven cases.
The user chose to retain 2 million paths and report this noise.
